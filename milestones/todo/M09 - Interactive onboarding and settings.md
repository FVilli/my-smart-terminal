# M09 - Interactive onboarding and settings

## Goal
Turn the first-run wizard into a real settings flow: re-open it from the app, navigate back and forward safely, verify the remote repository with a real commit/push, smoke-test AI and STT, and document every setup screen with linked guides.

## Exit criteria
All M09 tasks are done; settings can be opened from the main UI; the wizard never traps the user; remote registration is written and pushed (or fails with a recoverable explanation); AI and STT connection tests are available; guides are linked from the app and repository; tests and smokes cover the flows.
